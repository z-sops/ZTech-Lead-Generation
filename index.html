Tips for better results

  • Pair a trade with a place, for example plumbers and Baku.
  • Keep one specific keyword per run so results stay reviewable.
  • Start with a small quantity until the keyword looks right.
  • Open advanced filters only when you need a rating or website rule.
  • Save results to the Lead Library before starting the next run.
